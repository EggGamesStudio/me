HOW WE MAKE GAMES

We imagine it.
Then we get cracking.

Our own AI pipeline gets ideas out of our heads and into our hands, fast.

01

Shape the idea.

Our team defines the concept: the world, the characters, the mechanics, and the kind of fun we want to create.

02

The game developer builds.

Our game developer agent briefs specialist agents for screens, art, 3D, animation, and sound, then develops the game in Unity.

03

Play. Test. Pick the best.

We play every prototype, keep what’s fun, and take the most promising ideas into full development.

MEET OUR GAME DEVELOPER AGENT

One lead agent.
6+ specialists.

Our team works with one AI agent: the game developer. It sends each of its 6+ specialist agents a brief, checks what comes back, and then develops the game itself in Unity, from the mechanics to the levels and progression.

CURIOUS? GOOD.

A few things
to know.

Can I use Egg Games to make my own game?+

No. Our AI pipeline is an internal tool we use to develop our own games. It isn’t offered as a product or service.

What does the game developer agent do?+

It leads our AI pipeline. It talks with 6+ specialist agents, gives each one what it needs, checks the results, and then develops the game in Unity itself. Our team directs every step.

Does every game take 28 minutes and $5?+

No. Those are approximate figures from an internal demo of our prototyping pipeline. A finished game takes much more design, polish, and testing than a first prototype.
